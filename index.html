How much does a Chandigarh to Manali outstation cab package cost?

A round-trip Chandigarh to Manali cab package starts from ₹8,999 for a Swift Dzire and from ₹14,500 for a Toyota Innova Crysta, subject to travel dates and availability. The final quotation may vary depending on the vehicle, itinerary, tolls, parking, state taxes and other applicable charges.

Are toll taxes, parking fees, and state border taxes included in cab quotes?

The inclusions depend on the package selected. M.S. Tour & Travels provides transparent quotations showing the applicable vehicle charges, driver allowance, tolls, parking and state taxes where applicable. Ask for an itemized quotation before confirming your booking.
